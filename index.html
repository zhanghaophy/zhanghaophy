Research

Selected themes and recent publications.

Generalized symmetries across geometry and field theory

My work explores generalized and non-invertible symmetries, superconformal field theories, string compactifications, and the topology of gauge groups. My doctoral dissertation studied generalized symmetries in supergravity and superconformal field theories through string theory.
